Key Projects

Two-Stage Operational Amplifier Simulation & Layout

IIT Roorkee
  • Designed and characterized a two-stage operational amplifier with Miller compensation in Cadence SPECTRE.
  • Achieved differential gain >70 dB, GBP >100 MHz, phase margin >60°, and slew rate ≥0.1 V/µs.
  • Optimized for high CMRR, low power dissipation, and operation within a 1.8 V supply voltage, validating through RC-extracted layout simulations.

Transistor-Level FFT Design using Logical Effort

IIT Roorkee
  • Designed Radix-2 and Radix-4 FFT architectures at transistor level using logical effort methodology.
  • Optimized critical path delay and power using a custom 28T full adder design.
  • Improved throughput and speed for real-time DSP applications via efficient gate sizing.
